From scope to shipped.

A fixed four-step delivery cycle. You know what's next and what you'll have in hand at the end of each step.

01

Discovery

We map your current process or data, and define what "done" looks like in concrete terms before writing a line of code.

Deliverable: a written scope with the exact inputs, outputs, and edge cases covered.
02

Build

A working version goes up early. You test the real system, not mockups, from the first week, and can redirect before we're deep in.

Deliverable: a staging link you can click through and try with real inputs.
03

Deploy

We ship into your existing stack: your cloud, your tools, your auth, with documentation your team can actually follow without us in the room.

Deliverable: a live system plus a runbook covering how to change or extend it.
04

Support

A defined window of monitoring and fixes right after launch, with an optional retainer if you want continued iteration after that.

Deliverable: a fix-response window in writing, and a monthly summary if you're on retainer.
