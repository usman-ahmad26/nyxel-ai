What we build.

Two disciplines, each scoped and delivered by the same small team — no handoff between "the AI people" and "the people who actually ship it."

AI automation delivery

We replace the manual steps between your tools — the copy-pasting, the re-typing, the "someone checks this every morning" tasks — with pipelines that run on their own and flag you only when something needs a decision.

  • Built on n8n, LangGraph, or direct model + API integrations, whichever fits your stack
  • Triggers off events you already have: form submissions, new emails, CRM updates, webhooks
  • Every automation ships with a plain-language runbook, not just code
  • Human-reviewed logic at every branch — no black-box decision paths

Full-scale RAG applications

A retrieval-augmented system that answers from your own documents and data — not from whatever the underlying model happened to memorize. Built for the cases where a wrong answer actually costs you something.

  • Chat interfaces, internal knowledge tools, and customer-facing assistants
  • Retrieval grounded in your source documents, with citations back to them
  • An evaluation pass before launch to check for unsupported or hallucinated claims
  • Vector store and pipeline choices made for your data's actual size and shape, not a default template

Ongoing AI ops

After launch, models drift, data grows, and edge cases show up that weren't in the original scope. We keep watching accuracy and reliability so the system stays trustworthy months in, not just on launch day.

  • Scheduled evaluation runs against real usage, not just the original test set
  • Monitoring for latency, cost, and answer accuracy
  • Available as a monthly retainer alongside project work
