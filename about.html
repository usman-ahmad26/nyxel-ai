Nyxel is a small, hands-on delivery practice: every build gets direct attention, start to finish.

Nyxel is run by a Computer Science student in Lahore building a serious AI and full-stack portfolio alongside coursework: automation pipelines, RAG applications, and evaluation tooling, built and shipped rather than left as demos.

AI tools are part of the build process here, the same way they're part of most serious engineering work now. What isn't automated is the judgment: every architecture decision, every prompt, every data-handling choice is reviewed and understood before it ships, not generated and left unchecked.

That's the actual pitch: you're not getting a templated agency package, and you're not getting an unsupervised AI output either. You're getting a build that one person stands behind end to end.
